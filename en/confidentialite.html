Privacy policy

Ulmi test version · last updated: 24/11/2026

In short. Ulmi is the name of the product. The controller of your data is Arthur Nyssen, Rue de la Cité 5, boîte A021, 4000 Liège, Belgium. For any question: ulmi.contact@gmail.com.

This page explains what we do with your address if you ask to test Ulmi and if you ask to be told when it launches, and what the test version does on your computer. We do not sell or rent your address, and we do not lend it to anyone.

If you ask to test Ulmi

  • Who is responsible? Arthur Nyssen, Rue de la Cité 5, boîte A021, 4000 Liège, Belgium. Contact: ulmi.contact@gmail.com.
  • What data? Your email address, the type of your computer (Mac or Windows) and what you write to us; your tester ID (10 characters calculated from the address you gave us for the test, by a one-way calculation with a secret key: your address does not appear in the ID or in the code); the date of your code; if your code is deactivated, the date and the reason; if you send us your test report or your feedback, its content, which includes your tester ID.
  • What for? To reply to you; to give you access to the download (named sharing on Google Drive); to register you as a tester of the Gmail connection of the test version; to read your feedback in order to improve Ulmi; to give you a personal code to open the test version, and to be able to deactivate it; to detect a code used by someone other than the person we gave it to, by comparing the ID in a test report with the address it reaches us from.
  • On what basis? Your request to take part in the test (GDPR, art. 6.1.b), to reply to you, give you access, create and check your code and check the version. Our legitimate interest (GDPR, art. 6.1.f) in protecting a confidential test version against copying and sharing, for the public list of deactivated codes and for comparing test reports. Without an address, we cannot open the test to you; nothing else depends on it.
  • Your right to object. You can object at any time to what we do on the basis of our legitimate interest: write to us. You can also simply remove the “Tester ID” line from your report before sending it.
  • Who receives this data? Arthur Nyssen, and Google Ireland Limited, which hosts our mailbox, the download, the Gmail connection project and the small program that creates your code and keeps the “ID → address” table, and which also processes this data for its own purposes, under its own privacy rules (policies.google.com/privacy). Google may process data outside the European Union; it is responsible for this under those same rules. GitHub sees your computer's IP address each time Ulmi starts: see below.
  • For how long? Until the end of the test phase, 31/12/2027 at the latest. After that, we remove your accesses and delete your application emails and the table linking your ID to your address. If your code was deactivated because it was used by someone else, we keep a record of this deactivation as long as a challenge is possible or under way, at the latest until the same date. A deactivated ID remains in the public list until the codes are changed, and at the latest until the end of the test. If you also asked to be told when Ulmi launches, your address follows the section “Being told when it launches” below.
  • Your rights: to see, correct and delete your data, to limit its use, to object, to receive it in a common format; to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Authority (Belgium) or the CNIL (France). Write to us at ulmi.contact@gmail.com.
  • No automated decision is made about you. An old version stops opening for everyone at the same time; the deactivation of a code is always decided by Arthur Nyssen himself, never by a program.

What Ulmi does at startup. At startup, the test version reads two small public files on the Ulmi website (ulmi-app.github.io): the number of the current version and the list of deactivated codes. Ulmi sends no data about you. But, as with any web page, the site is hosted by GitHub, Inc. (United States), which sees your computer's IP address and the time of the reading, and records them for its own security, under its own rules (docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement): it does not act on our instructions and we have no access to this information. GitHub states that it adheres to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, recognised by the European Commission (adequacy decision of 10 July 2023). We chose this means to check the version without Ulmi sending anything.

Ulmi keeps on your computer, in its settings folder, your personal code, the date of its first launch and that of its last check. This information does not leave your computer. The instructions say where this folder is.

The public list of deactivated codes contains only tester IDs, never an address. For a person who does not have your code, an ID alone does not make it possible to recognise you. But the person to whom you might have given your code can recognise yours in it.

If you ask to be told when it launches

You give us your email address to be told when Ulmi launches. We use it for nothing else. You can unsubscribe at any time: reply STOP to any of our emails.

1. Who is responsible for your data?

Arthur Nyssen, Rue de la Cité 5, boîte A021, 4000 Liège, Belgium. For any question about your data: ulmi.contact@gmail.com. Ulmi is the name of the product. The controller of your data is Arthur Nyssen.

2. What data?

  • Your email address.
  • The record of your registration: the email you sent us (date, address, request sentence). It serves to prove that you gave us your consent.
  • The date of your unsubscription, if you unsubscribe.

We ask you for nothing else. Please do not send us anything more.

3. Why, and on what basis?

  • To tell you when Ulmi launches, by email. Basis: your consent (GDPR, art. 6.1.a). You give it by ticking the box and sending us your email, and you can withdraw it at any time.
  • To keep the record of your registration, so that we can prove you had given your consent and that we respected your unsubscription. Basis: our legitimate interest in being able to respond to a complaint or a dispute (GDPR, art. 6.1.f).

We do no profiling and make no automated decision about you. Our emails contain no tracking pixel or tracking link: we do not know whether you open them.

4. Is your address compulsory?

Yes, to be told: without an address, we cannot write to you. But registering is voluntary. Not registering has no other consequence: you will be able to buy Ulmi in the same way.

5. Who receives your data?

  • Arthur Nyssen, alone, for the purposes above.
  • Google. Our mailbox, the sharing of the download and the Gmail connection of the test version go through a Google account. Google Ireland Limited therefore receives your address and our exchanges. Google also processes them for its own purposes, under its own privacy rules (policies.google.com/privacy), for which it is responsible: it does not act only on our instructions. Google may process data outside the European Union; it is responsible for this under those same rules.

We do not sell or rent your address, and we do not pass it on to anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

6. For how long?

  • We keep your address until Ulmi launches, then for 6 months, and at the latest until 31/12/2027. After that, we delete it, unless you have become a customer: your address is then covered by the customers' privacy policy.
  • If you unsubscribe, we immediately remove your address from the mailing list: you receive nothing more. We only keep the record of your registration and your unsubscription, without using it, until the same deadline, then we delete it too.
  • If you ask us to delete your address, we delete everything, record included, unless a complaint or a dispute is ongoing at that time.

7. How to unsubscribe

Each email we send you reminds you how to unsubscribe: simply reply STOP, or write to us at ulmi.contact@gmail.com. No account, no password, no reason to give. We confirm by email that it is done.

8. Your rights

You can, at any time and free of charge:

  • withdraw your consent, as easily as you gave it: a STOP reply is enough. What we did before your withdrawal remains valid;
  • see the data we hold about you and receive a copy of it;
  • correct your address;
  • have your data deleted;
  • ask us to freeze it, while a disagreement is settled (restriction);
  • receive it in a common format to give it to someone else (portability);
  • object to the keeping of the record of your registration (point 3). We will delete it, unless we need it to respond to an ongoing complaint or dispute.

Write to us at ulmi.contact@gmail.com. We reply within one month at most. If we have doubts about your identity, we may ask you to write to us from the registered address.

9. A complaint?

Write to us first: we will do what is needed. You can also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of the country where you live, where you work, or where the problem occurred:

  • Belgium: Data Protection Authority (Autorité de protection des données / Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit), Rue de la Presse 35 / Drukpersstraat 35, 1000 Brussels · contact@apd-gba.be · www.dataprotectionauthority.be (complaint form in French, Dutch or German)
  • France: Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés (CNIL), Service des plaintes, 3 place de Fontenoy, 75007 Paris · online complaint at www.cnil.fr/fr/plaintes (in French)

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top of the page. If the change concerns how your address is used, we tell you by email before applying it, and we ask for your consent again if necessary.
